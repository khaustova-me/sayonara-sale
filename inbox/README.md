# Drop photos here

Put photos of things you want to sell in this folder — straight from your phone,
any size, any orientation, JPEG or HEIC. Then just say in chat which item they
are and what it costs.

**With several photos of one thing, give it its own folder:**

```
inbox/washing-machine/1-front.jpg
inbox/washing-machine/2-controls.jpg
inbox/washing-machine/3-model-label.jpg
inbox/sofa/...
```

Photos are used in filename order, and the first one becomes the cover photo —
the one people see in the grid. So put a number in front of the best overall
shot and it'll lead. Folders aren't required; a few loose photos are fine too,
and I'll work out which item is which by looking at them.

Nothing in here gets published. Only the cleaned-up, metadata-stripped copies
written into `images/` end up on the website.

Once an item is on the site you can delete its photos from this folder.
